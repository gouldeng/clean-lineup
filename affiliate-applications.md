# Affiliate application answers (Clean Lineup)

- Site: Clean Lineup, https://cleanlineup.com
- Contact: Michael Gould, Long Beach, California, USA, mike@gouldengineering.com
- Description: Clean Lineup is a free guide to water quality and conditions at 65 Southern California surf spots, from Santa Barbara to Imperial Beach. Each spot page combines official county beach advisories and bacteria grades, rain-advisory status, and live NOAA buoy water temperatures, then recommends what to wear for that day's water temperature (springsuit, 3/2 fullsuit, 4/3 with booties).
- Promotional methods: Text links and small product blocks in our own content: the "What to wear" section on each spot page, the home page, and a wetsuit-thickness guide. Plus a free weekly email newsletter. No paid search, no trademark bidding, no coupon/cashback, no pop-ups, no spam. Affiliate disclosure on every page with product links.
- Audience: Surfers, bodyboarders, swimmers and beachgoers in LA, Orange, San Diego, Ventura and Santa Barbara counties who check ocean safety and water temp before going out.
- Traffic: Just launched (October 2026), no meaningful traffic yet. 66+ pages with automatically updated data; growing through organic search and the newsletter.
- Where links appear: "Wetsuit picks" box on each spot page below live conditions; home page; About page thickness table; newsletter.
